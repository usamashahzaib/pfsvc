Freelancer verification services

PFSVC offers three services. Freelancer verification checks identity, skills and interview performance, then issues a public seal. Seal lookup lets clients confirm any badge ID. Team verification covers five or more members with an account manager and API access. Basic costs PKR 1,499 a year and Professional costs PKR 9,999.

Freelancer verification

For freelancers and remote professionals in Pakistan. Four steps, all done by people:

  1. Identity and document review against government-issued ID.
  2. Skills assessment for your discipline, run by certified examiners.
  3. Live video interview with a senior practitioner in your field.
  4. Seal issuance. The seal is publicly searchable and valid for 12 months.

Public seal lookup

Hiring managers enter a member's badge ID on the verification page. No login is needed. PFSVC confirms each verification within 24 hours.

Team and enterprise verification

For agencies and teams verifying five or more members. Includes a dedicated account manager, 48 hour turnaround, a custom verification page, a white-label certificate option and API access for credential checks. Pricing is quoted per team.

How PFSVC checks applicants

  • Government ID cross-check (CNIC or passport) and a live video liveness match.
  • Sanctions and AML screening.
  • Timed, proctored skills tests with browser lock and AI-detection tools.
  • A live interview with a PFSVC-certified senior practitioner in your field.
  • Annual re-verification. The seal is revoked if work is falsified or contracts are outsourced.
