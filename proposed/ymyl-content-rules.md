# YMYL content rules for pfsvc.com.pk

Financial pages can affect a reader's money. Search engines and AI answer engines hold them to a higher bar. These rules apply to every page and every PR.

1. Cite a primary source for every regulatory statement. Use SECP, SBP, or the text of the law or circular itself. Show the source URL and the date you checked it.
2. Make no promise about returns, savings, approval, or outcomes. Describe what the service is and what it costs. Do not say what the reader will gain.
3. Do not use "best", "#1", "guaranteed", or "risk-free".
4. State a licence, registration, or membership only when the regulator's public register lists it. Link to that register entry.
5. Put a real author on each page. Add a "Reviewed by" line and a review date only when a named person actually reviewed it.
6. Show a "Last reviewed" date that matches the real review. Update it only when the content is re-checked.
7. Open each answer with a plain 40 to 60 word response to the question in the heading.
8. Add FAQPage or other schema only when the same text is visible on the page. Never write Review or AggregateRating schema yourself.
9. Every PR for this site is titled "[REVIEW REQUIRED]" and is checked by the owner before merge.
10. Anything a person must decide, such as tax, legal, or investment choices, carries a line that the page is general information and not advice. Confirm the exact wording with a qualified professional.
